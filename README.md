## VOIS AICTE Major Project (2026–2027)

A comprehensive data analytics project focused on understanding how seasonal variations influence agricultural performance through the analysis of crop production, environmental conditions, resource utilization, and economic outcomes.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

Agriculture is one of the most season-dependent industries in the world. Changes in rainfall, temperature, humidity, soil conditions, water availability, and farming practices can significantly affect crop productivity and profitability.

Although large volumes of agricultural data are generated every year, identifying meaningful seasonal patterns from raw data is often difficult.

This project applies data analytics techniques to investigate how agricultural performance varies across different seasons and to uncover insights that can support better agricultural planning and decision-making.

The analysis explores seasonal differences in:

- Crop Yield
- Production Levels
- Revenue
- Profitability
- Water Usage
- Resource Efficiency
- Environmental Conditions
- Disease and Pest Risk
- Regional Agricultural Performance

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Agricultural activities are influenced by seasonal variations in environmental conditions, farming practices, resource availability, and market conditions.

As a result, agricultural performance may differ from one season to another.

However, raw agricultural data does not clearly explain how agricultural performance changes across seasons or what patterns can be observed under different seasonal conditions.

The objective of this project is to analyze agricultural data collected across multiple seasons and identify meaningful patterns, trends, relationships, and variations that affect agricultural performance.

# 📉 Statistical Techniques Applied

## Correlation Analysis

Used to measure relationships between agricultural variables.

### Objectives

- Identify strong positive relationships
- Identify strong negative relationships
- Discover influential variables

## ANOVA Testing

Used to determine whether seasonal differences are statistically significant.

### Null Hypothesis (H₀)

There is no significant difference in agricultural performance across seasons.

### Alternative Hypothesis (H₁)

At least one season differs significantly from others.
